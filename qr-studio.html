Questions people ask

Can I add my own business logo?

Yes. Upload a JPG, PNG or WebP logo. It sits in a white-backed centre badge, and we automatically switch to high error correction. Always scan-test your finished design before printing.

Does my QR code expire?

No. This makes a static QR code, with no redirect service. The destination link must still exist for scans to open it.

Are my images or content uploaded?

No. Your QR code and logo are rendered directly in your browser.
